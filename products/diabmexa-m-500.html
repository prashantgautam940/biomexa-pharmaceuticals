Your daily routine

When to take Diabmexa M 500 — and what to do the rest of the day.

  • 6–8 AM

    Morning dose with breakfast

    One tablet with breakfast and a glass of water. Never on an empty stomach.

  • 12–2 PM

    Check after-meal sugar

    No dose. Check your sugar 2 hours after lunch if you can; choose fibre, protein and whole grains.

  • 6–8 PM

    Evening dose with dinner

    One tablet with dinner, if your doctor prescribed twice daily. Always with food.

  • Next morning

    Check fasting sugar

    No dose at night. Check your fasting sugar before breakfast and log it with the AI Pharmacist.

Food & drug interactions

What to avoid, what to be careful with, and what helps.

Avoid

Alcohol

Raises the risk of lactic acidosis — rare but serious — and can cause low sugar. Avoid, or keep strictly minimal.

Avoid

Taking it on an empty stomach

Causes nausea, stomach upset and loose motions. Always take it with a meal.

Caution

Scans with contrast dye (CT, angiography)

Tell your doctor you take metformin — it may need to be paused for a short time around the scan.

Caution

Vitamin B12 (long-term use)

Metformin can lower B12 over the years. Your doctor may check your B12 level once a year.

Caution

Very high-fat meals

Slow down how the tablet is absorbed. Prefer lighter, balanced meals.

Good for you

High-fibre foods

Oats, dal, vegetables and whole grains slow sugar absorption and work well with metformin.
