Lead Campaigns

Upload a list of new contacts (from clinics, pharmacies, camps or your website) and invite them on WhatsApp to start free medicine reminders. People who tap Start Reminders get the sign-up link automatically; Stop removes them for good. When a lead signs up, they move to Joined on their own.

1. Upload leads

CSV with a header row and columns name and number (phone / mobile also works). Numbers like 98765 43210, +91… or 0987… are fixed automatically. Duplicates, invalid numbers and people already on Biomexa are skipped. Download sample CSV

2. Send WhatsApp invite

Sends your approved lead_onboarding template to waiting leads, oldest first. To protect your number's quality rating, only 250 go out per 24 hours, between 9 AM–9 PM India time.
