Health tips with Telmexa AM

Small daily habits that help your medicine work better.

Do

  • Take it at the same time every morning
  • Check your BP at home a few times a week and log it below
  • Eat more vegetables, fruit, dal and whole grains
  • Stand up slowly, especially in the first 2 weeks
  • Walk for 30 minutes on most days

Avoid

  • Grapefruit and grapefruit juice
  • Salty foods — pickles, papad, chips, packaged snacks
  • Potassium supplements or salt substitutes, unless your doctor advises
  • Alcohol — it adds to dizziness and raises BP
  • Stopping the tablet on your own, even when your BP looks normal
When to get help: Get medical help right away if you notice swelling of the face, lips or throat, difficulty breathing, fainting, or chest pain.
