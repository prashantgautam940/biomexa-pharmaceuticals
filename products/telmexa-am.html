Your daily routine

When to take Telmexa AM — and what to do the rest of the day.

  • 6–8 AM

    Take your tablet

    One tablet with breakfast and a full glass of water. Morning dosing covers the natural early-morning rise in BP.

  • 12–2 PM

    Check your BP

    No dose needed. Check your BP if you can, keep lunch low in salt, and drink enough water.

  • 6–8 PM

    No evening dose

    Telmexa AM is taken once a day only. Don't take a second tablet in the evening.

  • 10 PM

    Rest well

    Aim for 7–8 hours of sleep — poor sleep can push your BP up.

Food & drug interactions

What to avoid, what to be careful with, and what helps.

Avoid

Grapefruit & grapefruit juice

Raises amlodipine levels in the blood, which can cause very low BP, dizziness and ankle swelling. Avoid completely.

Avoid

High-salt foods

Pickles, papad, chips, processed and packaged foods work against the medicine. Keep sodium under 2 g a day.

Caution

Alcohol

Adds to the BP-lowering effect and can cause dizziness or fainting. Best avoided — if you drink, keep it minimal.

Caution

Potassium supplements & salt substitutes

Telmisartan can raise potassium. Avoid supplements or "low-sodium" salts unless your doctor advises; your doctor may check potassium levels.

Good for you

DASH-style diet

Vegetables, fruit, dal, oats, nuts and fish support healthy BP alongside your medicine.

Good for you

Beetroot & pomegranate

Heart-friendly foods that fit well into a BP-friendly diet.
