Help & support

Something not working? Report it here and you'll get a ticket number straight away. Our team replies on WhatsApp — usually within one working day. You can also email biomexapharmaceuticals@gmail.com.

🔒 Your report is only seen by the Biomexa support team. Never share passwords or OTP codes in a ticket.

Report a problem

For patients, doctors and IVF centres: missed reminders, login trouble, reports, payments or anything else.

Prefer email, or need to send a screenshot or file? Write to biomexapharmaceuticals@gmail.com — add your ticket number if you have one.

Check a ticket

Enter your ticket number and the WhatsApp number you used.

Quick fixes

I'm not getting WhatsApp reminders

Send "Hi" to the Biomexa WhatsApp number once — WhatsApp only lets us message people who have chatted with us. Then check the reminder time in your patient portal under My medicines.

I forgot my password

Use "Forgot password?" on the login page, or send RESET to the Biomexa WhatsApp number to get a code.

My reminder came at the wrong time

Reminder times are in Indian time. Open My medicines in the patient portal, stop the medicine and add it again with the right time. If it still happens, raise a ticket.
