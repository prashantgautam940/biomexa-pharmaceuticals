Health tips with Diabmexa M 500

Small daily habits that help your medicine work better.

Do

  • Always take it with your meal, never on an empty stomach
  • Check your sugar regularly (fasting and after meals) and log it below
  • Fill half your plate with vegetables; choose whole grains and dal
  • Walk after meals — even 10–15 minutes helps
  • Keep your doctor informed about any other diabetes medicines you take

Avoid

  • Alcohol — it raises the risk of a serious reaction (lactic acidosis)
  • Skipping meals after taking your dose
  • Sugary drinks, sweets and refined flour (maida)
  • Stopping the tablet on your own
  • A CT scan with contrast dye without telling the doctor you take metformin
When to get help: Get medical help right away for unusual muscle pain, trouble breathing, severe stomach pain, or feeling very cold and weak — or if your sugar falls below 70 mg/dL with shakiness or confusion.
