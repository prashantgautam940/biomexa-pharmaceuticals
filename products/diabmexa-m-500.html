Drug-Food Interactions

Alcohol

Metformin + alcohol increases risk of lactic acidosis — a rare but life-threatening condition. Alcohol also causes hypoglycemia and masks warning symptoms. Avoid completely or strictly limit.

High-Fat Meals

Delays metformin absorption by 35% and reduces peak plasma concentration. Take with low-fat meals for consistent glucose control.

Iodinated Contrast Media

Must discontinue 48 hours before and 48 hours after contrast studies. Risk of acute kidney injury and lactic acidosis.

Vitamin B12-Rich Foods (Long-term)

Metformin reduces B12 absorption by 30% over time. While B12 foods are healthy, monitor serum B12 annually. Consider supplementation after 2+ years.

High-Fiber Foods (Recommended)

Oats, lentils, vegetables slow glucose absorption, complementing metformin's mechanism. Best taken together with medication.

Cinnamon & Fenugreek

Natural insulin sensitizers that may enhance metformin efficacy. Add to breakfast taken with morning dose.
