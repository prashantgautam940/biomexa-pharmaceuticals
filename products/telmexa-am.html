Drug-Food Interactions

Grapefruit & Grapefruit Juice

Amlodipine is metabolized by CYP3A4. Grapefruit inhibits this enzyme, increasing amlodipine plasma levels by up to 3x, risking hypotension and edema. Avoid completely.

High-Sodium Foods

Excess sodium counteracts telmisartan's ARB mechanism. Processed foods, pickles, and canned soups can raise BP. Limit sodium to <2g/day.

Alcohol

Enhances hypotensive effect. May cause dizziness, fainting. If consumed, limit to 1 standard drink and take medication 2 hours apart.

High-Potassium Foods

Telmisartan can raise serum potassium. Large amounts of bananas, spinach, and supplements may cause hyperkalemia. Monitor levels monthly.

DASH Diet (Recommended)

Leafy greens, berries, oats, fatty fish, and nuts enhance drug efficacy. Take with a light, low-fat meal for optimal absorption.

Beetroot & Pomegranate

Natural nitrates support vasodilation, complementing amlodipine's mechanism. Best consumed in morning, 1 hour before dosing.
