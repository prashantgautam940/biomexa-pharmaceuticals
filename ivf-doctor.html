The gap between visits

The problems every IVF centre knows, and how Biomexa closes them

Each card shows a problem. Hover over it, or tap it, to see the fix.

  • The problem💉

    Missed or mistimed injections

    Stimulation doses are skipped and trigger shots go in late, and you often only find out at the next scan.

    See the fix
    With Biomexa

    Every dose reminded and confirmed

    • You set the medicines; patients get WhatsApp reminders
    • Trigger shot reminded at the exact time
    • Patients tap Taken or Not taken, and you see it live
  • The problem🚨

    OHSS warning signs reported late

    Rapid weight gain, bloating or breathlessness reach you days later, sometimes only in casualty.

    See the fix
    With Biomexa

    Red flags triaged for you

    • One-minute daily check-in on weight, pain, bloating, bleeding, fever
    • Rules flag OHSS signs, fever and heavy bleeding by severity
    • Critical patients rise to the top of your list
  • The problem📄

    Reports scattered across chats

    Hormone values and scan reports arrive as photos on personal WhatsApp, mixed with everything else.

    See the fix
    With Biomexa

    One review queue, with an AI head start

    • Patients upload reports straight into their cycle
    • An AI draft reading is ready when you open it
    • You review, comment, and the patient is notified
  • The problem📞

    Endless calls from anxious patients

    "Is this pain normal?" calls interrupt your OPD, and the two-week wait is the hardest time of all.

    See the fix
    With Biomexa

    Structured follow-up, on your schedule

    • Secure messages linked to the patient's cycle
    • Patients request video, audio or chat follow-ups
    • You confirm a time that fits your day
  • The problem📉

    No picture between scans

    You see a snapshot at each visit, with no record of how the patient was in the days between.

    See the fix
    With Biomexa

    The whole cycle on one screen

    • Stage, dates and medicines for each patient
    • Weight and symptom trends across the cycle
    • Doses, check-ins and reports in one history
  • The problem🧭

    Patients feel alone and drift away

    Between visits, patients feel unsupported. After a failed cycle, many never come back.

    See the fix
    With Biomexa

    Daily contact that builds trust

    • A daily touchpoint from your centre, every stage
    • Patients choose your centre from your profile
    • Full history kept for the next cycle

How follow-up works

From the patient's phone to your dashboard in five steps

Drag to turn, or pick a step below.

🤝Step 1

Patient joins your centre

PatientPicks your centre on Biomexa and gives consent.

YouAccept the request and set stage, dates and protocol.

💊Step 2

You set the medicines

PatientGets a WhatsApp reminder for every dose and the trigger shot.

YouSee each dose marked Taken or Not taken.

🌡️Step 3

Daily check-in

PatientLogs weight, symptoms and vitals in about a minute.

YouRed flags are scored by severity and sent to your alerts.

📄Step 4

Reports and review

PatientUploads blood tests and scans from home.

YouRead the AI draft, add your comment, mark it reviewed.

🎥Step 5

Virtual follow-up

PatientRequests a video, audio or chat follow-up.

YouConfirm the time, follow up and update the plan.

  • ⏱️Act earlierWarning signs reach you the same day, not at the next scan.
  • 🎯Better adherenceReminders and confirmations for every dose, including the trigger.
  • 🗂️Less adminReports, messages and follow-ups in one place, not five chats.
  • 💗Patients who stayDaily contact that keeps patients with your centre.
